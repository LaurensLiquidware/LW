SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The PowerShell script itself (Set-ProfileUnitySplashScreenLogo.ps1) contains no
third-party components. It binds only to assemblies that ship with Windows as
part of the .NET Framework -- PresentationFramework, PresentationCore,
WindowsBase, System.Windows.Forms and System.Drawing -- which are provided by
the operating system and are not redistributed here. The embedded brand artwork
(the Liquidware wordmark, the flame app icon and the hex texture) is first-party
Liquidware material derived from the Liquidware design system.
